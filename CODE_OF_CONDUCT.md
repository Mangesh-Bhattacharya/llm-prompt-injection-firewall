# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Critique code, benchmarks, and ideas — not people.

**Expected**
- Assume good faith; ask clarifying questions before concluding something is wrong.
- Back technical claims with a test, a repro input, or a link.
- Accept corrections gracefully and credit others' work.

**Not acceptable**
- Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, or doxxing.
- Spam, unsolicited recruiting/promotion, or off-topic self-promotion in issues and PRs.
- Publishing a detection bypass publicly before the maintainer has had a chance to respond — use the private process in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, lock threads, and block accounts that violate this policy. Report concerns privately via GitHub (repository **Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability** for security matters, or by contacting the maintainer through their GitHub profile). Reports are handled confidentially and acknowledged within 72 hours.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) v2.1.
