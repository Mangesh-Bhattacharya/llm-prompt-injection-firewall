      How it works

      Every request is scored by three independent layers, then mapped to a verdict.

      ALLOW — score below 25
      FLAG — ambiguous, logged for review
      BLOCK — score at or above 50, high-confidence attack

      Integrate in your own app

      Same detector, three ways in. Pick whichever fits your stack.
